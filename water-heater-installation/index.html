Water Heater Installation in Marlboro, NJ

Choose your water heater before the old one fails. We install gas and electric tank heaters in Marlboro with correct venting and drainage.

Planned water heater installation gives you time to choose the right unit instead of whatever is available during a failure. We install tank water heaters in Marlboro homes, and we plan the venting, drainage and safety piping so the new heater runs correctly for years.

Use this page when you are replacing an aging heater before it fails, moving one, or adding capacity for a household that has grown.

What a good installation includes

  • ✓Correct supply connections with shutoff valves you can reach.
  • ✓A temperature and pressure relief valve with proper discharge piping.
  • ✓Venting that matches the fuel type and is sealed and sloped correctly.
  • ✓A drain pan or drainage path where a leak would cause damage.
  • ✓An expansion tank where the plumbing is a closed system.

Planning the install

We start with where the old heater sits and what connects to it. Moving a heater from a closet to a basement wall changes pipe runs, venting and sometimes the electrical or gas connection, so the plan matters more than the swap itself.

Then we match the size to your household. Counting bathrooms and people at peak hours gives a better answer than copying the nameplate on the old unit.

Many Marlboro homes were built with gas service, and others use electric or propane, so check which connection you have before shopping. We confirm the existing setup during the visit and tell you what a change would involve.

Permits and inspections

Whether a permit is required depends on the work. Plumbing in Marlboro Township is reviewed by the Construction Division before a Certificate of Occupancy, and a final inspection is required for new work. Installation work follows New Jersey's plumbing subcode, which adopts the National Standard Plumbing Code as its model code (N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15). For replacements and larger changes, ask the division at (732) 536-0200, extension 1800.

We explain what we have seen required on similar jobs, and we arrange the work so an inspector can see the finished connections.

Getting ready for installation day

  • ✓Clear a path to the old heater and the area around it.
  • ✓Know where the main water shutoff is and whether it works.
  • ✓Tell us if anyone in the household relies on hot water at set times.
  • ✓Have the model number of the old heater handy, or send us a photo of its label.
  • ✓Decide whether you want the old tank removed.

A little preparation shortens the visit. We also use the walk-through to check the shutoff valves, since an old valve that will not close can turn a two hour job into a longer one.

After the install, we show you the temperature setting, the relief valve and the drain valve. Draining a few quarts from the tank once a year helps clear sediment, and we explain how to do it safely.
