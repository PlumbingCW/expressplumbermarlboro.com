Route 9, the mall and the neighborhoods behind them

Freehold Raceway Mall opened on Route 9 in 1990, and the corridor around it holds stores, restaurants and offices. Businesses like these produce grease, constant restroom use and tight closing times, which makes after hours plumbing calls common for a retail strip.

Behind the corridor, residential streets run out to places such as Adelphia, Smithburg and Siloam, and to East Freehold and West Freehold. Homes there range from postwar houses to large newer developments, so we ask about the street and the house age on every call.

How growth shaped Freehold Township plumbing

Freehold Township grew by around 176 percent during the 1960s, and it has kept growing since. That mix of postwar and recent housing means lines from several eras sit side by side, sometimes within a single block.

Postwar houses often have galvanized or early copper supply and cast iron or clay drains. Recent construction usually has PEX and PVC. A leak or a clog in one is very different from the other, so a quick description of the pipe material helps us bring the right parts.
