Sewer Camera Inspection in Marlboro, NJ

A camera inside the pipe replaces guesswork. We inspect Marlboro sewer lines for roots, cracks and sags, and explain what we see.

A sewer camera inspection is a look inside your drain line using a waterproof camera on a flexible cable. In Marlboro it answers the question that cleaning cannot, which is why a line keeps backing up, and it is also the sensible check before buying an older home.

The camera shows cracks, sags, offset joints, roots and buildup on a monitor while we work, so you see the same picture we do.

What the camera can find

Roots in the line

Fine root hairs at a joint look different from a dense mat, and the repair differs too.

Belly or sag

A low spot that holds water and waste is a common reason for repeat clogs.

Cracks and breaks

Fractured or collapsed sections show up clearly, along with the soil around them.

Offset joints

Pipe sections that have shifted apart create a ledge that catches paper and wipes.

How an inspection goes

We open the cleanout, feed the camera toward the street or septic system, and record as we go. A locator at the surface follows the camera head, so we can mark the depth and position of any problem before anyone digs.

If a blockage is in the way, we may clear it first with a cable machine so the camera can pass. You receive a plain explanation of what we saw and, if repair is needed, the realistic options.

Before you buy or after a backup

Homes in Marlboro range from decades old to recent, and a house with large trees on the lot can hide a root problem that a visual walk-through will never show. An inspection before closing tells a buyer what the line looks like on the inside.

After a backup, the camera tells you whether the event was a one-time blockage or a sign of damage. That difference decides whether you need sewer line cleaning or sewer line repair.

What happens after the inspection

The camera tells you what is wrong, and the next step depends on what we find. A clean pipe with no defects means the earlier problem was a blockage, and you can go back to normal with a note about what to keep out of the drains.

If the camera shows buildup, we discuss cleaning or jetting. If it shows damage, we show you the location and depth and go over the repair choices. Nothing is decided on the day unless you want it to be.

Related services and towns

After an inspection, we can move straight to drain cleaning or hydro jetting if buildup is the cause. Our cameras travel to Marlboro, Colts Neck and Matawan.
