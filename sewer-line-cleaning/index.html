Sewage in the basement or a toilet that will not stop gurgling is an emergency, and it usually means the main sewer line is blocked. Emergency sewer line cleaning clears the pipe that carries all of your household wastewater from the house to the street or septic system, and we can reach Marlboro addresses at any hour.

If sewage is coming up through a floor drain, keep everyone away from the area and call us before you try anything else.

Main line versus house drain

A house drain serves one fixture or a branch. The main sewer line collects every branch and carries it out of the house. When the main is blocked, the lowest fixtures show it first, so a basement floor drain or tub is usually the first to back up.

That pattern is the clue we use on the phone. If your upstairs sinks run fine but the basement drain overflows, we plan for a main line cleaning.

How we clean a main sewer line

We find the cleanout, usually in the basement, crawl space or yard, and feed in a heavy duty cable with a cutting head. For roots and thick blockages, the head grinds the mass down so water can pass.

Once flow returns, we run a camera when the situation calls for it. That shows whether the blockage was a one-time event or a symptom of a crack, a sag or roots entering at a joint. See our sewer inspection page for what the camera reveals.

Why Marlboro sewer lines clog

Mature trees are part of what makes Marlboro's neighborhoods attractive, and their roots are a common cause of main line blockages. Roots look for moisture and enter at joints, then grow into a dense mat that catches paper and wipes.

Wipes sold as flushable are the other repeat offender, along with grease poured down kitchen drains. Homes on septic systems have a different risk profile, and those owners should ask the township or a septic professional about the tank before blaming the pipe.
