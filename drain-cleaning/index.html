If wastewater is rising in a tub, a floor drain or a toilet, stop running water in the house and call us. Emergency drain cleaning means removing a blockage from a household drain line so wastewater flows away again, and in a Marlboro home it is often the difference between a mop-up and a flooded basement.

A single slow sink can wait for a daytime appointment. Several fixtures backing up or gurgling together usually means the main line is blocked, and that needs a plumber now.

When a clogged drain is an emergency

Water in more than one fixture

A tub that fills when the washer drains, or a toilet that bubbles when a sink runs, points to the main line.

Sewage smell indoors

A strong odor near a floor drain or basement bath means wastewater is moving the wrong way.

Standing water that will not drop

If a plunger and an hour of patience change nothing, the blockage is deeper than the trap.

Backup after heavy rain or a long shower

Timing helps us decide whether the cause is inside the house or out in the yard line.

How we clear a drain

We start by asking which fixtures are affected, because that tells us where the clog sits. A bathroom sink that is slow while everything else works is a branch problem. A basement floor drain that wells up is a main line problem.

For branch lines we run a cable machine with a head sized to the pipe. For the main line we use a larger cable, and when the cable alone leaves a film of grease or scale, we recommend hydro jetting to scour the pipe wall.

If the same drain keeps clogging, a camera shows why. A sewer camera inspection can reveal a sag, a crack or roots that no cable will fix for long.

What clogs drains in Marlboro homes

Many Marlboro houses date from the 1980s and early 1990s and have several bathrooms, a laundry room and a finished basement all feeding one main. More fixtures means more hair, soap film and wipes arriving at the same few drain junctions.

Kitchen lines carry cooking grease that cools and hardens inside the pipe. Older tree-lined lots add another cause, because roots find the small gaps at joints and grow into the line over several seasons. Whether your home is on public sewer or a septic system changes who handles what, so confirm that with the township before assuming.

What to do until we arrive

  • ✓Stop running water, including the washer and dishwasher.
  • ✓Do not pour chemical drain cleaner into a standing backup, because it can splash back and harm the pipe.
  • ✓Keep people and pets out of any room with sewage on the floor.
  • ✓Move rugs and stored boxes away from the affected drain.
