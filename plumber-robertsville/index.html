Robertsville and its place in the township

Robertsville is a named community in Marlboro Township and a census-designated place, which means it is a locality on the map and not a separate town. Addresses here are Marlboro Township addresses.

The name appears in everyday township business. Robertsville Elementary School opened in 1968, and the township's master plan lists Robertsville Fire Company 2 and refers to the Robertsville and Greenbriar intersection.

A crossroads with some history

A stagecoach station once stood at the intersection of County Route 520 and Tennent Road. County Route 520 is Newman Springs Road, one of the two main roads that cut across the township.

The history is a good reminder that this part of Marlboro has been settled for a long time, while much of its housing arrived in the later growth decades. The township master plan reports that about two thirds of the housing stock counted in 2000 was built in the 1980s and 1990s.

Drains, sewers and basements

We do not have records of the sewer lines or foundations in Robertsville, so we describe the work in general terms. A house built any decade can have a blocked sewer lateral, a failed sump pump or a slow drain. What matters is finding out which one you have.

  • ✓Slow drains in more than one fixture often point to a main line blockage.
  • ✓A gurgling toilet when the washer drains is another common sign.
  • ✓A basement floor drain that backs up is a reason to stop using water.
  • ✓Water along a basement wall after rain may be a pump or drainage problem, not a plumbing leak.
