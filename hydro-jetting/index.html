When a drain keeps clogging no matter how many times it is cabled, the problem is usually a coating on the pipe wall that a cable only punches through. Emergency hydro jetting uses a high pressure water hose and a special nozzle to scour grease, scale and silt off the inside of the pipe, and we can bring it to your Marlboro property when a line is packed solid.

This is the page to read if your kitchen line, laundry line or main drain has been cleared several times and keeps coming back.

Backed up again? Why jetting beats another cabling

  • ✓The drain flows for a week or two after cleaning and then slows again.
  • ✓A cable comes out with grease, soap film or thick sludge on it.
  • ✓The line is a large one, such as a main or a laundry stack, and a cable leaves a narrow channel.
  • ✓A camera shows a pipe wall lined with buildup rather than one clear blockage.

How the process works

A jetting hose is fed into the line through a cleanout, and the nozzle fires water backward and sideways as it advances. The spray cuts the buildup free and flushes it toward the sewer, which is why the pipe ends up looking close to new on camera.

We usually look with a camera first. That shows whether the pipe is sound enough for pressure, and it lets us show you the before and after on the monitor.

Jetting is not right for every line. A cracked or collapsed pipe can be damaged by high pressure, and a heavy root mass may need a cutting head first. We tell you honestly when a repair from our sewer line repair service makes more sense than more cleaning.

Where jetting helps in Marlboro

Homes built during the 1980s and early 1990s boom often have long kitchen runs and several bathrooms feeding a single main. After decades of use, those lines carry a layer of grease and mineral film that cabling only tunnels through.

Restaurants and shops along Route 9 and Route 79 face the same problem faster, because commercial kitchens send far more grease down the line. We handle these through commercial plumbing calls as well.
