Greenbriar at Marlboro, a 55 and older community

Greenbriar at Marlboro is an age-restricted community in Marlboro Township for adults 55 and older. A local real estate guide describes it as 766 single-family detached homes built from 1981 to 1992, located between Routes 9 and 18. The township's master plan also names it, in a note about the Robertsville and Greenbriar intersection.

Listings for the community use streets such as Suffolk Way, Murray Hill Terrace, Lakeview Drive and Caldwell Terrace. The community is organized under its own association, so residents should read their own rules before any exterior or shared plumbing work.

What plumbing help looks like for adult community homeowners

Many residents want a plumber who arrives when promised, explains the problem without jargon and does not need them to climb into an attic or crawl space. We cannot promise arrival times, but we can promise to explain the cause and the price before work starts.

We do not know the plumbing inside these homes, and the homes were built over about a decade. Water heaters, toilets and faucets are replaced over time in any house, so the age of a given fixture varies from home to home.

  • ✓Water heater leaks or no hot water.
  • ✓Toilets that run, clog or leak at the base.
  • ✓Faucets and shutoff valves that drip or will not close.
  • ✓Slow drains in kitchens and baths.
  • ✓Garbage disposals that jam or hum.

Planning ahead so a repair is not an emergency

  1. 1

    Find your shutoff

    Know where the main water valve is and make sure it turns easily.

  2. 2

    Note the water heater age

    The data plate has a serial number that shows when it was made.

  3. 3

    Keep contact numbers handy

    Post the plumber, the association manager and the utility on the refrigerator.

  4. 4

    Ask about exterior rules

    Check what the association requires before any work that changes the outside of the home.
