Where Morganville sits in Marlboro Township

Morganville is a named community inside Marlboro Township. It is a census-designated place and not a separate town, so a Morganville address is a Marlboro Township address for taxes, schools and permits. Mail here uses ZIP code 07751, while most of the rest of the township uses 07746.

The area has a railroad past. Morganville was one of the stops on the Monmouth County Agricultural Railroad, which was completed in 1877. Today the landmarks are different, with the High Point Schools campus, Shalom Torah Academy and several residential subdivisions.

The kinds of homes we are asked about

Morganville has subdivisions of different ages. A local real estate guide describes Georgetown Estates, off Pleasant Valley Road, as about 87 custom single-family homes built in the early 2000s. It describes Country Hills as a smaller subdivision near Route 34 and Lloyd Road, and Chelsea Square as a condominium-style community for adults 55 and older, established around 2007.

We have no public records about the pipes inside any of these homes, so we do not guess at them. The practical point is simple. Newer construction and older construction both need the same first step when something goes wrong, which is to find out what is actually happening before anything is replaced.

    Close the main shutoff

  2. 2

    Switch off power to wet areas

    Do this only if you can reach the panel without standing in water.

  3. 3

    Protect what you can

    Lift rugs and boxes off wet floors and photograph the damage for insurance.
