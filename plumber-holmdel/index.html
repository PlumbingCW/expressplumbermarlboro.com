A broken water line or a failed water heater is hard to ignore, and in Holmdel it often involves a large house with many fixtures. Our emergency line is open around the clock, and we dispatch a licensed plumber from Marlboro to Holmdel addresses on both sides of Route 34 and Route 35.

First, find out who supplies your water

Holmdel residents are served by more than one arrangement. New Jersey American Water operates the Shorelands System that serves Hazlet, Holmdel and Union Beach, drawing on seven wells in the Old Bridge and Farrington aquifers plus water purchased from the Swimming River Reservoir.

The utility's 2023 water quality report lists total hardness between 60 and 140 ppm. For the exact figure at your tap, check the latest report or test the water yourself. For billing and service questions, New Jersey American Water customer service is at 1-800-272-1325.

Why this matters for plumbing: water with more dissolved minerals leaves scale in water heaters and on fixtures faster. It is one reason we check heating elements and anode rods on older Holmdel tanks before they fail.

Large homes, long runs and what fails first

Many Holmdel properties are big houses on wooded lots, often with several bathrooms, a finished basement and a laundry room on an upper floor. A supply line failure upstairs can send water through two floors before anyone notices.

Long drain runs are the other pattern. A sewer lateral that crosses a large lot is more likely to meet roots and settling, and sewer line cleaning followed by a camera is the usual path after a backup.
