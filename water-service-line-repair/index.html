The water service line is the buried pipe that brings water from the street connection to your house. When it breaks, you may lose pressure, see a wet or sunken yard, or notice a sudden jump in your bill, and the problem is urgent because the leak runs all day and night.

Emergency water service line repair means locating the break, digging only where needed, and replacing the damaged section or the line itself. We handle this for Marlboro properties at any hour.

Signs the service line has failed and why to act now

Low pressure everywhere

If every faucet runs weak, the problem may be at the service line or the meter, not a single fixture.

A wet or sinking patch of yard

Water rising to the surface along the line path suggests a break.

A bill that jumps

Constant flow underground raises the meter reading.

Sound of rushing water

A hiss or flow noise near the meter when everything is off is a warning sign.

Who is responsible for what

The utility is responsible for the main in the street and its equipment. The homeowner is generally responsible for the line from the meter to the house, but where exactly the dividing point sits can vary.

Ask the Marlboro Township Water Utility Division at (732) 536-0188 where its responsibility ends for your address before you dig. Its 2025 Water Quality Report states that there are no lead service lines in the township's territory, but you can still ask the utility about the pipe material serving your home.

How we repair or replace a service line

We start by confirming that the leak is on your side and locating it with acoustic tools and a line locator. That limits digging to the spot that needs it.

From there the options are a spot repair or a longer replacement. A spot repair suits one damaged section. If the pipe is old and brittle along its length, replacing the whole run avoids a second dig in a few years. We explain which makes sense and why.

Digging in Marlboro yards

Marlboro lots often have mature landscaping, irrigation and driveways over the line path. We mark the route first and keep the trench narrow, and we restore the area as agreed.

Before any excavation, call 811, the free utility locate service, so buried gas, electric and other lines are marked. Permit rules vary by the kind of work, so check with the Marlboro Township Construction Division at (732) 536-0200, extension 1800.
