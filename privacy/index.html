Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you call, email or browse this website.

Information we receive

This website has no forms. When you call us, we receive your phone number and whatever you tell us, such as your address and a description of the plumbing problem. When you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message.

We use this information to answer your question, schedule and carry out work, and keep records of the service we provide.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you prefer not to be recorded, tell the person who answers at the start of the call.

Third party content on this site

Pages on this site load fonts from Google Fonts and, on some pages, an embedded Google Map. Those services may receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser loads them. Their handling of that data is covered by their own policies.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by emailing contact@expressplumbermarlboro.com or calling (732) 466-1098.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always the one on this page.
