A toilet that is overflowing or leaking onto the floor needs attention right now. Emergency toilet repair means stopping the water, clearing or fixing the cause and, when the fixture is past saving, installing a new toilet, and we do it for Marlboro homes at any hour.

Reach behind the toilet and turn the small oval handle on the supply line clockwise to stop water flow. That one step prevents most of the damage while you wait for us.

Overflowing toilet: stop the water first

  1. 1

    Lift the tank lid

    If the bowl is rising, lift the flapper or float inside the tank so water stops entering.

  2. 2

    Close the supply valve

    Turn the valve behind the toilet clockwise until it stops.

  3. 3

    Do not flush again

    Another flush only adds water to a full bowl.

  4. 4

    Call us

    Tell us whether other drains are slow, because that points to a bigger blockage.

If the toilet and the nearest sink or tub back up at once, the blockage is not in the toilet. Read about drain cleaning and sewer line cleaning for what that means.

When to replace the toilet

A cracked bowl or tank cannot be repaired, and a toilet that needs a new flush valve every few months is wasting your money. Older toilets can also use far more water per flush than current models, which shows up on the bill.

When we install a new toilet, we check the flange, the floor around it and the shutoff valve. A rotted floor or a corroded valve is better fixed during the swap than discovered later.

Toilets in Marlboro homes

Many Marlboro houses have three or four bathrooms, so a toilet failure is rarely the only thing running on that line. When several fixtures share a stack, one overflow can travel through the drains and show up elsewhere.

That is why we ask which other fixtures are affected. A single bathroom problem is a toilet job, while a house-wide slowdown points to the main line.

Installing a new toilet: what we check

A good installation depends on what is under the toilet. We inspect the flange, the closet bolts and the floor, and we reset or replace the flange if it is cracked or sits too low.

We then seat the toilet on a new wax ring, level it, connect a fresh supply line and test flushes. You also get a tour of the shutoff, so you know where to close it next time.
