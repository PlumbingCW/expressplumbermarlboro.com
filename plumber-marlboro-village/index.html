What people mean by Marlboro Village

Marlboro Village is the historic center of the township, today known as the Main Street area, at the crossing of Route 79 and School Road. The township's own planning documents say the early township was made up of small hamlets that centered on the Village.

Those same documents describe the area today as a suburban place that is not pedestrian friendly, which fits a stretch of busy road with homes and businesses set back from it. It is a real place that locals recognize, though its edges are not drawn in the sources we found, so we describe it by its crossroads and not by exact boundaries.

Why a village core can mean mixed plumbing needs

Near a busy state route, a call can come from a house, an office, a shop or a restaurant. The plumbing is the same family of equipment, but the stakes differ. A leak in a house can wait an hour while you find the shutoff. A leak in a food business can close the doors.

We do not know the age or pipe material of any particular building here, and we will not guess. What we can offer is a calm first step, which is a clear description of what you see and a plan for stopping the water.
