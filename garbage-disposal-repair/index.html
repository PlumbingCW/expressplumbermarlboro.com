A garbage disposal that hums but will not spin, leaks under the sink or has backed up into the dishwasher side can stop a kitchen in its tracks. Emergency garbage disposal repair means freeing a jam, fixing a leak or replacing the unit, and we do it for Marlboro kitchens at any hour.

Before you do anything, turn the disposal's power off at the wall switch and the breaker. Never put your hand inside the disposal.

Disposal problems that cannot wait and what they mean

Humming but not spinning

The blades are jammed. A plumber can free them with a hex key from underneath.

Nothing at all, no hum

The reset button on the bottom or a tripped breaker is the first thing to check.

Leak under the sink

The seal, a flange or the dishwasher connection may be failing.

Sink backs up or smells

The drain line past the disposal may be clogged.

What to try, safely

  • ✓Switch the power off first, at the wall and the breaker.
  • ✓Look for an object stuck in the grinder with a flashlight, then remove it with tongs, never with a hand.
  • ✓Press the red reset button on the bottom of the unit.
  • ✓Insert a hex wrench in the bottom socket and work it back and forth to free the blades.
  • ✓If water pools in the sink, call us, because the drain beyond the disposal may be blocked.

Kitchens in Marlboro and what goes down the drain

Large Marlboro kitchens with islands and second sinks often have a disposal working as hard as the main one. Fibrous vegetable scraps, bones, grease and starchy foods like rice and pasta are the usual reasons for jams and clogs.

A disposal is not a trash can. Running cold water while it works and keeping grease out of the drain keeps both the unit and the pipe beyond it clear. If the sink drain itself is slow, see our drain cleaning page.

Installing a new disposal: what we check

Replacing a disposal sounds simple, but a few details decide whether it works well. We check that the sink flange is sound, that the mounting assembly fits and that the electrical connection is safe, either a plug or a hardwired connection.

We also reconnect the dishwasher drain correctly, including the knockout plug that must be removed on a new unit. Skipping that step is a common reason a dishwasher drains poorly after an installation.
