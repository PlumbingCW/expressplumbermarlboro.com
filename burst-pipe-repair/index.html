When a pipe bursts, every minute lets more water into your walls and floors. Emergency burst pipe repair means stopping the flow, repairing or replacing the failed section, and helping you limit the damage, and our line is open around the clock for Marlboro addresses.

Read the first three steps below now if water is flowing. They matter more than anything else on this page.

Do these three things first

  1. 1

    Shut off the main

    Close the main water valve, usually near the meter or where the line enters the house.

  2. 2

    Cut power to wet areas

    If water is near outlets or the panel, turn off the breakers safely, but do not wade through water to reach them.

  3. 3

    Open a low faucet

    Open a faucet at the lowest level to drain remaining water, then call us.

If you cannot find or turn the main valve, call us anyway and we will help you find it. For a leak on the street side of the meter, call the Marlboro Township Water Utility Division at (732) 536-0188 or, after hours, Marlboro Township Police at (732) 536-0100.

Why pipes burst

Frozen water expands and splits copper, PEX and galvanized lines, usually at a joint or in an unheated space such as an exterior wall, garage or crawl space. Corrosion and old fittings fail under normal pressure, and a water hammer from a quick-closing valve can break a weak spot.

In Marlboro winters, a hard cold snap puts every pipe in an exterior wall at risk. A pipe that froze overnight may not leak until it thaws, which is why damage often shows up in the afternoon.

What we do when we arrive

We confirm the water is off and find the failed section. Then we cut out the damaged pipe and replace it with a new length, using fittings that match the pipe material and your pressure.

We also check the rest of the run for weak spots, because a pipe that froze once often has other damaged sections. When a long stretch is corroded or damaged, we explain whether repiping makes sense instead of repeated patches.

After the repair: protect your home

  • ✓Photograph the damage before cleanup for your insurance claim.
  • ✓Remove standing water and open the area to air.
  • ✓Dry wet drywall, carpet and insulation quickly to limit mold.
  • ✓Call a water damage specialist if more than a small area is wet.
  • ✓Insulate exposed pipes in unheated areas before the next freeze.
