Terms of Service

The terms that apply when you use this website or call us for plumbing service.

Using this website

The information on this site is general guidance about plumbing and is not a substitute for an inspection of your own system. Every home is different, and a plumber needs to see the problem before giving advice that fits it.

Scheduling and pricing

Appointments are made by phone. Same-day appointments are offered when available and depend on workload and the nature of the problem. A clear price is given before work starts, and any change in scope is explained to you before it goes ahead.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Emergencies

If anyone is in danger, call 911. If you smell gas, leave the building and call your gas utility or 911 before calling a plumber.

Limits

We do our best to keep the information on this site accurate, but we cannot promise it is complete or current for your situation. Permit and code requirements are set by your local authorities, so confirm them with the Marlboro Township Construction Division before a larger project.

Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent to contact@expressplumbermarlboro.com.
