Who we are

A plumbing line for Marlboro, answered all day and night

Marlboro Express Plumbing is based at 2 Hudson St in Marlboro, New Jersey. We exist for one reason: when water is where it should not be, you should reach a person quickly and hear a plain plan for fixing it.

We take calls for emergency repairs and for planned plumbing work. Emergencies get the fastest appointment we can offer for your address, and planned jobs are scheduled for a time that works for your household.

How we work

Every job starts with a question about safety. If water is spreading, we help you reach the main shutoff. If you smell gas, we tell you to leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 before anything else.

After that we find the cause rather than guessing. For drains that means a camera or the right cable. For leaks it means listening and electronic tools before anyone cuts into a wall. Then you hear the price, in writing if you like, before the repair begins.

What we cover

Our plumbers handle drain cleaning, water heater repair, leak detection, sewer line repair, sump pumps and boiler repair, along with the smaller fixture jobs that fill a normal plumbing week. See the full list on the services page.

We serve Marlboro Township, including Morganville, Robertsville and Wickatunk, plus Manalapan, Freehold Township, Holmdel, Colts Neck and Matawan.

How to check any plumber

Licensing is public information. Before you let any plumber start work, ask for their New Jersey license number and verify it through the New Jersey Division of Consumer Affairs. A real plumber will not mind the question.

For larger jobs, also ask whether a permit is needed. Plumbing work in Marlboro Township is reviewed through the Construction Division, which you can reach at (732) 536-0200, extension 1800. The state plumbing subcode, N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15, adopts the National Standard Plumbing Code as its model code, and that is the standard our installation work is expected to meet.

What emergency service means for us

Emergency does not only mean a pipe is spraying. It means a problem that gets worse by the hour or that puts your home or your safety at risk. Sewage in a basement, a leaking tank, a sump pit that is filling and no heat in winter all fit.

For those calls, the first goal is to limit damage. We stay on the phone while you close valves and clear the area, and we send a plumber who is equipped to fix the cause, not just to look at it.

Pricing in plain terms

You hear the price before work starts. The price depends on access, parts, the time of day and what the plumber finds. If a second problem turns up, we stop and explain it first, so you are never surprised by something you did not approve.

We do not use scare tactics. If a repair can wait, we tell you so and explain what to watch for.

What we ask of you

Two things make an emergency visit go faster. First, know where your main water shutoff is before you need it, and make sure it turns. Second, if you can, take a photo of the problem and have it ready, because it helps us bring the right parts.

If you rent, tell us who owns the building. If you own, keep the paperwork for your water heater handy, because the label and the date on it save time when we choose a repair.

Local knowledge that matters

Marlboro's housing ranges from older homes in the village area to large developments built from the 1970s through the 1990s and newer estates. That means a mix of pipe materials, water heater ages and drain layouts, and we plan each job around what is actually in your house.

We also keep the local contacts handy. Water utility questions go to the Marlboro Township Water Utility Division at (732) 536-0188, and permit questions go to the Construction Division at (732) 536-0200, extension 1800.
